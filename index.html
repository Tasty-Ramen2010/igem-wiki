Hard to use

5.5 ÅFlexPepDock only works if you start that close to the answer. You need the answer to get the answer.

  • AlphaFold-Multimer only says whether a peptide binds. Its scores (pLDDT, PAE, ipTM) take structural-modelling expertise to read.
  • HADDOCK 2.4 gives a binding score, but is much slower, and works about 45% of the time even when the binding region is roughly known.
  • FlexPepDock needs a starting model that is already close to the answer (within 5.5 Å).

Sources: our Description page, London et al. 2011

We faced this problem with MALARIA

Most rapid tests look for HRP2, made by a gene the parasite can delete. About 2 in 10 cases go undiagnosed, a gap the WHO has formally acknowledged.

The test itselfTwo ideas make one malaria test.

Idea 1

LOCKR

A protein cage that glows when it meets PfLDH. The enzyme opens the latch, a split luciferase snaps together, and with furimazine it makes light.

How it works →

Idea 2

Coffee-ring assay

As a droplet of sample dries, the flow inside carries the glowing cages to its edge and stacks them into a bright ring you can see by eye.

How it works →

Future steps

Malaria is the first target, not the last.

This pipeline works for any disease. Design peptides with de novo design software, then test them with HybriDock-Pep to find the best one. Use LATTE to scan and optimise the binder for grafting into LOCKR, and to predict how much LucKey gives a target signal, all before ordering anything.
